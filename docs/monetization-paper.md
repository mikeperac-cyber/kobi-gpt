# KOBİ-GPT — Monetization Paper (draft v1)

Status: draft. Every number is either **sourced** (with a link or report name) or **assumed** (marked *assumption*). Assumptions must be tested with real customers before this paper is used in a pitch.

## 1. What we sell

KOBİ-GPT helps small Turkish manufacturers start exporting: a multilingual storefront for buyers, a compliance check with a suggested customs code (GTİP), and drafted buyer outreach that the producer approves before anything is sent.

Who pays: the producer (owner of a 5–50 person factory). Who benefits but does not pay in v1: the foreign buyer.

## 2. Market

- **Sourced:** KOSGEB reports **131,928 exporting SMEs in 2023**, up from 54,948 in 2013 (KOSGEB, *Türkiye'de KOBİ'lerin Dış Ticaretteki Yeri (2025)*).
- **Sourced:** Turkey exported above USD 1 billion from 31 provinces in 2024 (T.C. Ticaret Bakanlığı press release, 2025).
- **Assumption:** Most of the target is the non-exporting SMEs that want to start. Not yet counted; needs a source before the pitch.

**Serviceable market used below:** 131,928 exporting SMEs. Starting with exporting SMEs is deliberate: they already pay for compliance and buyer search, so the willingness to pay is proven.

## 3. Pricing (proposed — all *assumption* until tested)

| Plan | Who | Price (EUR / month) | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Start** | One product, one market (DE) | 40 | Storefront, 1 compliance report per quarter, 20 buyer messages |
| **Pro** | Up to 10 products, one market | 90 | Unlimited reports and messages, PDF documents, analytics |
| **Partner** | Chamber, association or consultant | 300 per month + per-member fee | White-label onboarding, dashboard for members |

Add-ons (one-off): **expert review of a compliance report**, **extra market** (per country), **curated buyer-list pack**.

Annual prepayment: 2 months free (standard SaaS discount, *assumption*).

## 4. Revenue scenarios (year 1–3)

Formula: paying firms × monthly price × 12, plus add-ons (not included below, so these figures are a floor).

| Scenario | Paying firms | Blended price (EUR/mo) | Yearly revenue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early (year 1) | 300 | 50 | ≈ 180,000 |
| Base (year 2) | 2,000 | 60 | ≈ 1,440,000 |
| Strong (year 3) | 10,000 | 65 | ≈ 7,800,000 |

Share of exporting SMEs needed: Base = 1.5% of 131,928; Strong = 7.6%.

**Reading:** the Base case needs roughly 1 in 66 exporting SMEs to pay. That is a plausible but not safe share; it depends on partner channels (chambers, consultants) more than on direct sales.

## 5. Costs (per year, rough)

| Item | Assumption |
|---|---|
| AI calls | ≈ €1–2 per firm per month (cheap model; scales with usage) |
| Hosting, database, email provider | ≈ €100–300 per month at 2,000 firms |
| Compliance knowledge (expert verification) | ≈ €100–300 per rule-set update, per product category |
| Sales and partner management | the largest cost; one person from year 2 |
| Payment fees | ≈ 2–4% of revenue (card processor, *assumption*) |

Gross margin before sales: roughly 85–90% (*assumption*, driven by AI and hosting costs).

## 6. Channels (how the paper's numbers get reached)

1. **Chambers and exporter associations** (TİM members and regional chambers): one partner deal can bring hundreds of members. *Assumption:* partner deals close slowly.
2. **Export consultants** and customs brokers referring clients.
3. **Direct and content** (LinkedIn, Turkish trade press): slow, cheap, low volume.
4. **Government programme eligibility:** check whether SME export-support programmes can cover part of the fee. Not verified.

## 7. Before we can charge (blocking items)

From the project roadmap, these must exist before paid use:
- **Billing and plan limits.** Nothing exists yet: no subscriptions, no usage meter.
- **Real outbound email after approval.** Today approval opens the producer's mail app; it does not send.
- **Verified compliance coverage.** Seed rules are marked demo/unverified. Paid reports need expert sign-off per product and country.
- **Real freight rates.** Current logistics rates are labelled examples.
- **Legal:** terms of service, data-processing agreement (KVKK), and a refund policy.

## 8. Risks

- **Trust:** a wrong customs code or rule could cost a producer money. Mitigation: every rule shows its source and date; GTİP is a suggestion checked by a customs broker.
- **Free alternatives:** state platforms may offer similar features free of charge. Our differentiator is the storefront and buyer outreach, not the checklist alone.
- **Currency:** prices in EUR feel high to Turkish SMEs during inflation. Test a TRY price.
- **Channel dependence:** if chambers do not sign, the Base case slips by a year or more.
